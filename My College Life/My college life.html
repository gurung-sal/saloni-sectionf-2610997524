• College Terminology

Lecture
A class where a teacher explains a particular subject or topic to students.
Semester
A division of the academic year during which students complete a specific set of courses.
Assignment
A task or piece of academic work given to students to complete and submit.
Examination
A formal test used to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding of a subject.
Attendance
The record of a student's presence in classes or college activities.
