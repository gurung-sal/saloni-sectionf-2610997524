Famous Places in India

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal in Agra is a world-famous monument known for its beautiful white marble architecture.

India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial and landmark in New Delhi.

Jaipur

Jaipur is known for its royal palaces, forts and colourful culture.

Kerala

Kerala is famous for its backwaters, greenery, beaches and beautiful natural scenery.

Goa

Goa is popular for its beaches, Portuguese heritage and relaxed atmosphere.
