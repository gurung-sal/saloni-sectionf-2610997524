Tourist Destinations

Explore some of the most popular tourist destinations in India. Each destination offers a different experience, from mountains and beaches to forts, palaces and cultural attractions.

1. Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for its mountains, valleys, rivers and pleasant weather.

Manali is a popular destination for families, couples and adventure lovers. Visitors can enjoy beautiful natural scenery and many outdoor activities.

Popular Places to Visit in Manali

  • Solang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Hadimba Temple
  • Manali Mall Road
  • Vashisht Hot Springs
  • Old Manali

Things to Do in Manali

  • Enjoy the mountain views
  • Visit beautiful valleys
  • Enjoy snow activities
  • Go trekking
  • Visit temples
  • Explore local markets

Best For

Manali is best for families, honeymoon couples, nature lovers and adventure travellers.

2. Goa

Goa is one of India's most popular beach destinations. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, warm weather, delicious food and relaxed atmosphere.

Goa is a great destination for people who want to enjoy beaches, sightseeing, water activities and local culture.

Popular Places to Visit in Goa

  • Baga Beach
  • Calangute Beach
  • Anjuna Beach
  • Palolem Beach
  • Fort Aguada
  • Panaji

Things to Do in Goa

  • Relax on the beaches
  • Enjoy water sports
  • Visit old churches
  • Explore local markets
  • Enjoy Goan food
  • Watch beautiful sunsets

Best For

Goa is suitable for families, friends, couples and people who enjoy beaches and relaxing holidays.

3. Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its magnificent forts, palaces, colourful markets and rich culture.

Jaipur is a great destination for people interested in history, architecture, traditional food and Indian culture.

Popular Places to Visit in Jaipur

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Nahargarh Fort
  • Jal Mahal

Things to Do in Jaipur

  • Visit historical forts
  • Explore beautiful palaces
  • Shop in local markets
  • Try traditional Rajasthani food
  • Learn about local culture
  • Take photographs of historical buildings

Best For

Jaipur is best for families, history lovers, culture lovers and people interested in architecture.

4. Shimla

Shimla is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh. It is known for its pleasant weather, green mountains and colonial architecture.

Popular Places

  • The Ridge
  • Mall Road
  • Jakhu Temple
  • Kufri
  • Christ Church

5. Kerala

Kerala is known for its beautiful backwaters, beaches, greenery and traditional culture. It is a peaceful destination for families and nature lovers.

Popular Places

  • Alleppey
  • Munnar
  • Kochi
  • Kovalam
  • Thekkady
