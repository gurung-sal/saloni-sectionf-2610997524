––––––––––––––––––––––––––––– CHITKARA UNIVERSITY , PUNJAB –––––––––––––––––––––––––––––



Chitkara University, Punjab is a well-known private university located in Rajpura, Punjab, and is known for providing a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes in areas such as engineering, computer science, business, design, health sciences, hospitality, and applied sciences.

The university focuses on combining academic learning with practical experience so that students can develop both theoretical knowledge and skills required for their future careers. Its campus provides modern classrooms, laboratories, libraries, recreational facilities, and spaces for student activities.

Students can participate in various clubs, societies, competitions, cultural events, sports, workshops, and other extracurricular activities, giving them opportunities to explore interests beyond academics. Chitkara University also places importance on innovation, entrepreneurship, technology, communication skills, and industry exposure.

The university has collaborations and connections with industries and other educational institutions, which can provide students with opportunities to gain practical experience and understand professional environments.

With its diverse academic programmes, active campus life, and emphasis on skill development, Chitkara University provides students with an environment where they can learn, experiment, build confidence, and prepare themselves for further studies and professional careers.

FACLITIES :

Library

A quiet and well-equipped place where students can read books, study, borrow academic resources, and access useful reference materials for their courses.

Laboratories

Specially equipped spaces where students can conduct experiments, perform practical work, and apply the concepts they learn in their classrooms.

Cafeteria

A comfortable place where students can enjoy meals, snacks, and refreshments while relaxing and spending time with their friends between classes.

Sports Complex

A recreational facility where students can participate in various indoor and outdoor sports, exercise, and take part in physical activities.

Auditorium

A large and well-equipped hall used for seminars, workshops, cultural programmes, guest lectures, presentations, and other college events.
