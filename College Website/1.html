-------------------------------------------------------CHITKARA UNIVERSITY---------------------------------------------------


Chitkara University is a private university in Rajpura, Punjab, offering a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, health sciences, design, hospitality, and more.
It is widely recognized in North India for its industry‑aligned programs and strong academic reputation. It operates major campuses in Punjab and Himachal Pradesh, with the Punjab campus located directly in Rajpura, along the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway (NH‑44)
